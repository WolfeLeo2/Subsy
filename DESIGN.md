# Design System & Direction: Subsy 💳

## 1. Product & Visual Identity
- **Concept**: Clean financial cockpit.
- **Tone**: Calm utility and clarity, balanced with lively and playful elements.
- **Character**: Premium and trustworthy, yet accessible and tactile.

## 2. Color Palette & Surfaces
- **Theme Mode**: Adaptive Light & Dark (Material 3 Expressive dynamic color integration).
- **Surface Elevation**: Tonal elevation using M3 surface containers rather than excessive floating drop-shadows.
- **Brand Financial Accents**:
  - `BrandGreen` (`#10B981`): Healthy status, confirmations, active free trials under control.
  - `BrandRed` (`#EF4444`): Imminent trial expiry (within 3 days), cancellations, urgent alerts.
  - `BrandAmber` (`#F59E0B`): Upcoming renewals, warnings.
  - `BrandBlue` (`#3B82F6`): Work & SaaS, informative status.
  - `BrandPurple` (`#8B5CF6`): Personal, gaming, entertainment presets.
- **Restraint Rule**: Accent colors are reserved for actionable moments, category identity markers, and urgent trial alerts.

## 3. Typography & Numerical Hierarchy
- **Font Family**: Google Sans Flex (variable font).
- **Numerical Figures**: Large, punchy currency amounts and burn-rate totals with tabular figures.
- **Whitespace & Breathing Room**: Generous padding and structural whitespace around financial metrics to ensure fast readability at a glance without visual clutter.

## 4. Antislop Design Dials
- **ENERGY**: `2.5` (Balanced to expressive — punchy financial hero metrics, crisp contrast, no flat sterile voids).
- **RHYTHM**: `2.5` (Hierarchical cards — hero monthly burn rate card at the top, varied card treatments for free trials vs. recurring subscriptions).
- **MOTION**: `2.5` (Fluid Material 3 Expressive spring physics, tactile touch responses, smooth animated segmented filters).
